:class:`~filelock.SoftFileLock` and :class:`~filelock.SoftFileLease` use the inode saved at acquisition to remove their
marker. This closes a signal window during release that left the marker behind after the lock reported itself unlocked.
On POSIX, an error unlinking the marker propagates to the caller. A filesystem that refuses to unlink an open marker
still gets a retry after close.
A signal handler can force-release a newly published marker and raise :exc:`SystemExit`. The interrupted acquire
leaves that descriptor closed.
