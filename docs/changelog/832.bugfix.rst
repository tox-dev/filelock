:meth:`AsyncReadWriteLock.close() <filelock.AsyncReadWriteLock.close>` and
:meth:`AsyncSoftReadWriteLock.close() <filelock.AsyncSoftReadWriteLock.close>` on a singleton wrapper end only the holds
taken through that wrapper, a reader arriving during ``close()`` is refused instead of joining the hold being closed, and
the async wrappers stop admitting readers into a hold that synchronous code closed or force-released.
