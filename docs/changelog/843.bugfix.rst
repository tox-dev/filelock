Fix :class:`~filelock.SoftFileLock`, :class:`~filelock.MarkerSoftFileLock` and :class:`~filelock.SoftFileLease` removing
a live holder's marker when a freed inode number was handed to the next marker, when a stalled
:class:`~filelock.FileLock` removed its ``ENOSYS`` placeholder after a peer had replaced it, or when a failed acquire
cleaned up after a successor took the path. Restoring a marker a stale break moved aside no longer follows a symlink
found under the break name, and works without ``os.link`` or hard link support.
